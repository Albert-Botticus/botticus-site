The big four

The four things every parent asked for first.

PowerSchool, ParentVue, ClassDojo, Canvas, Google Classroom, the lunch account, the district email blast. The Bot logs into all of them and sends one morning digest: grade changed, assignment missing, slip due Friday, picture day, lunch balance low. A calendar that routes itself

Practices, games, rehearsals, early dismissals, the orthodontist. Pulled from TeamSnap, GameChanger, school calendars and emails, then pushed to the right person's calendar with the right lead time. The kid gets the 30-minute reminder. You get the "who's driving" question the night before, not the morning of.

Kids can talk to it

Each kid gets their own thread, by text message from a phone on your plan or on a family device. "When's practice?" "What's for dinner?" "Tell Mom I'll be late." The Bot answers, relays, and logs. You see everything. Kid mode means no purchases, no outside contacts, no open internet, and a fixed list of things it will do.

Lemonade stand, lawn mowing, baking, the robotics team, a portfolio. The Bot builds a real page in minutes, a parent approves it before it goes anywhere, and it lives on your family's own site. First name only, no photos of the kid, no school, no address. A first business with training wheels.

Kids, safely

How a Bot talks to children in your house.

This is the part we are strictest about, and the rules below are not optional extras. They are how every family Bot is built.

Your accounts, your rules, your eyes

  • A parent sets up each child's thread. Only phone numbers and devices you approve can reach the Bot.
  • Kids reach it by text message from a phone on your plan or on a family device. We do not put children on Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord or any chat app with its own age rules.
  • Every conversation with a child is visible to the parents. Nothing is private from you.
  • Kid mode is a fixed list: schedule, reminders, relaying messages to family, homework nudges, chores. No purchases, no new contacts, no browsing, no money, no exceptions the kid can talk it into.
  • The Bot says it is a Bot. Every child's thread opens with that, in plain words.

What we promise about their data

  • The Bot runs inside accounts your family owns. Botticus does not collect, store or sell information about your children. Anything the Bot knows about a child came from a parent.
  • Children's Bots run on the AI provider's commercial platform with its child-safety guidance applied: content filtering, monitoring, and parental oversight, in line with the provider's guidelines for organizations whose products interact with minors and with children's privacy law, including COPPA.
  • A child's website carries a first name only. No photos of the child, no school, no address, no last name. A parent approves it before it goes live, and can take it down with one message.
  • You can read, change or delete every rule and every memory. They are files you own.
