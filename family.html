A family runs on a thousand small tasks. Hand them to the Bot.

Four school portals, three calendars, two kids who need a ride at the same time, a dentist who only books by phone, and a permission slip due tomorrow. The same Bot that runs a business runs this, because to the Bot it is the same job: read everything, remember everything, do the routine part, and ask you only when it matters. It lives in your accounts, answers to you, and teens can text it under your rules. Younger kids never talk to it; you do, and it handles their logistics.

The fun one

Tickets, presales, drops and restocks.

The things that sell out in four minutes while you're making dinner. Concert tickets and presale codes first: your Bot knows when the presale opens, has the code ready, and buzzes you the second the queue goes live. Then the rest: a Pokémon Center release, a sneaker raffle, the OHLQ bourbon lottery, a NeeDoh restock, a preorder at the local game shop. Whatever opens, your phone buzzes with the link.

  • Tickets first. It tracks the artists and venues you care about, catches the presale announcement and the code email, and reminds you ten minutes before the queue opens, with the link, so you're in the line at 9:59 instead of 10:04.
  • Watches what you tell it to watch. A product page, a brand's email list, a store's socials, a lottery calendar. It checks around the clock so you don't.
  • Enters where the rules allow. One-entry lotteries like OHLQ, preorders, waitlists: it submits yours the moment they open, in your name, once.
  • Never cheats a queue. No bots on sites that ban them, no duplicate entries, no resale games. A ban would be yours, so it doesn't earn you one. On those sites it gets you to the front of the line fastest, then hands you the phone.

Kids and teens, safely

Who talks to the Bot, and who doesn't.

This is the part we are strictest about, and the rules below are not optional extras. They are how every family Bot is built.

Your accounts, your rules, your eyes

  • Children under 16 never interact with the Bot. It handles their schedules, portals and reminders through you, the parent.
  • From 16, a parent can set up a teen's Telegram or WhatsApp thread, where the app's own age rules allow it. Only accounts you approve can reach the Bot.
  • Every conversation with a teen is visible to the parents. Nothing is private from you.
  • Teen mode is a fixed list: schedule, reminders, relaying messages to family, homework nudges, chores. No purchases, no new contacts, no browsing, no money, no exceptions they can talk it into.
  • The Bot says it is a Bot. Every teen's thread opens with that, in plain words.

What we promise about their data

  • The Bot runs inside accounts your family owns. Botticus does not collect, store or sell information about your children. Anything the Bot knows about a child came from a parent.
  • Where a teen under 18 can talk to a Bot, it runs on the AI provider's commercial platform with its guidance for users under 18 applied: content filtering, monitoring, and parental oversight, in line with the provider's guidelines for organizations whose products interact with minors and with children's privacy law, including COPPA.
  • You can read, change or delete every rule and every memory. They are files you own.
