What it does

Everything between "order came in" and "box went out."

📥

Orders arrive on their own

Every ten minutes it reads the order emails from Shopify, Etsy and TikTok Shop and puts the order on the pack list, with product photos. Shopify can also connect directly for exact line items.

📦

Scan-to-pack

Tap Pack, scan each product's QR as it goes in the box. A checklist ticks off every item with a progress bar. The pick list under each product (screws, anchors, caps, bag, card) turns green without scanning each one. A product that isn't in the order gets a warning beep.

➖

Supplies deduct themselves

Each product knows what it consumes, down to grams of filament and one polybag. Scanning the product deducts all of it. The order completes itself when everything is scanned.

🔔

Reorder before you run out

Counts in the units you buy: bags, packs, spools. Hit the reorder point and an email goes out immediately, plus a daily digest of everything low, each with your saved one-tap reorder link and the tracking once it ships. Printed parts say "print more" instead.

🏷️

QR labels for every bin and product

Print on sticker paper. Each QR opens the app straight to that bin or product, so any phone camera can scan it. A $25 handheld scanner works too and is fastest for packing.

🚚

Inbound shipments matched

Shipping emails from the Shop app and suppliers are matched to what's on order, and the tracking link shows up in the app next to the item it's for.

🔍

Search by part

Type "long screw" and see every product that uses one, with live stock. Every product page lists exactly what goes in its box.

🧾

A log by person

Every change: stock used, received, ordered, printed; packing started, cancelled, done; orders arriving. Filter by person, action, date or text. The full history stays.

🔐

Only your people get in

Google sign-in with a short allowlist of accounts plus a password, lockout after repeated failures. Your packer never needs access to the spreadsheet underneath.

Where it lives

Your Google account. Nobody else's.

A Google Sheet holds the data. A small web app, running in your account, is the screen your phone or scanner talks to. Gmail supplies the orders and the tracking. There is no vendor, no monthly licence, and no server to patch. If you ever stop working with us, every row, label and log stays exactly where it is, in your account, working.
