Timing matters

Three reasons Amanda might move your filler appointment

  1. You have been sick in the past couple of weeks and needed antibiotics.
  2. You had dental work or a cleaning in the past two weeks.
  3. You just got a vaccine.

Each of these revs up your immune system and can cause inflammation. Waiting a couple of weeks keeps inflammation low and gets you the best result. If any of these apply, just let us know and we will find a better time. It is worth the wait.
