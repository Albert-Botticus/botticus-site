How it works

  1. Pick your tier online in a couple of minutes.
  2. Your monthly payment banks as credit you can spend on any service or skincare.
  3. Member pricing applies on everything, every visit.
  4. Your balance never expires while your membership is active.
Start saving now

The fine print

  • 3-month minimum commitment, then cancel any time with 30 days’ written notice by email. No cancellation fee.
  • $1 = $1 credit toward services or retail. Banked funds are non-refundable and cannot be turned into gift cards.
  • Member pricing does not apply to specials, coupons, sales, promotions or model pricing, and cannot be combined with other discounts. Evolus Rewards can be used any time.
  • If you cancel, you have 90 days to use your remaining balance.
  • Pricing may change; members are notified in advance.
