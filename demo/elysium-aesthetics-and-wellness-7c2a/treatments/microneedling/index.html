Microneedling

Ultra-fine sterile needles trigger your skin’s own collagen and elastin. The result is smoother, firmer, healthier-looking skin that keeps improving for weeks.

What it treats

  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Acne scars and other textural irregularities
  • Enlarged pores
  • Uneven tone, pigmentation and sun damage
  • Dull or aging skin and mild laxity
  • Stretch marks

What to expect

About 30 to 45 minutes. Mild redness, like a sunburn, for 24 to 72 hours. Minimal downtime for most people, with gradual improvement as collagen rebuilds.

Amanda usually recommends a series of 3 to 4 treatments spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. Consistency is cute.
