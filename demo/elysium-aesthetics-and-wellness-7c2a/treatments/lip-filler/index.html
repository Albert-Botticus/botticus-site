Your appointment, step by step

  1. Free consultation. We talk through your goals, concerns and medical history to make sure lip filler is right for you.
  2. Preparation + treatment. Before photos, 20 to 30 minutes of numbing, consent forms, then treatment. The procedure itself takes about 30 to 45 minutes and you see results immediately.
  3. Aftercare + follow-up. Detailed aftercare instructions, then a follow-up 3 to 4 weeks later to check your result and refine if needed.

Mild swelling or tenderness is normal and usually settles within a few days. Results typically last 6 to 18 months, depending on the product and your metabolism.
