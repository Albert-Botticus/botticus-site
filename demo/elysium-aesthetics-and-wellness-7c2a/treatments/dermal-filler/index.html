What to expect

Consultation and facial mapping. A licensed medical provider assesses your structure, balance and volume, then builds a plan around your goals.

Treatment time. About 60 to 90 minutes depending on the areas treated.

Results. Visible immediately and refined over the following days as the filler settles. Results typically last 6 to 18 months.

Downtime + aftercare

Most clients have minimal downtime. Mild swelling, tenderness or bruising at the injection sites is common and usually resolves within a few days.

For a short time after treatment you may be asked to avoid strenuous exercise, excessive heat and pressure on your face.
