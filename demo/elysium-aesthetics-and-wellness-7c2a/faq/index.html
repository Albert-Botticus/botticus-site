Do you take insurance?

No. Elysium is a cash-pay practice. That gives you transparent pricing upfront, more privacy, more choice, quicker access to care and a more personal, unhurried experience.

Do you accept HSA or FSA cards?

Yes. Rules vary by plan, so we cannot guarantee a transaction will be approved or reimbursed. Check with your plan provider.

How does pricing work?

Every price is listed upfront, with no hidden fees or insurance surprises. Beauty Bank members get 10 to 15% off services and retail, and monthly payment plans are available through Cherry.

How do I pay?

Cash, credit and debit cards, and mobile payment apps.

Is a consultation required?

Yes. Every treatment starts with a consultation and medical assessment so the plan is safe and right for you. Lip filler consultations are free.

Why might my filler appointment be moved?

If you have been sick and needed antibiotics, had dental work or a cleaning in the last two weeks, or just had a vaccine, your immune system is busy and inflammation is more likely. Waiting a couple of weeks gets you a better result. Just let us know.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows forfeit the $50 deposit. You can call during business hours or change the appointment yourself online. Package terms are given at purchase.

What is your refund policy?

Services already rendered are not refunded. If you are unhappy with a treatment, please reach out. We take every concern seriously and will work with you on a resolution.

Will you teach me to do my own injections?

Yes. For GLP-1 or hormone therapy patients, our provider can visit your home to walk you through it for $50 per visit. Most patients find our step-by-step instructions are all they need.

What is the difference between a compounding and a commercial pharmacy?

Compounding pharmacies tailor medications to the patient. The formulations are not FDA approved, but the hormones used are FDA approved and come from FDA-inspected facilities, and compounding pharmacies are regulated by state pharmacy boards. Commercial pharmacies dispense only FDA-approved, mass-produced formulations.

Can men be treated at Elysium?

Absolutely. Men get tox and filler too, and our men’s health program offers TRT and sermorelin therapy.
