About Elysium

A sanctuary for your best self

Elysium is a husband-and-wife practice in the Polaris area of Columbus, built on clinical experience, honest advice and results that still look like you.

Owner + founder · Aesthetic injector

Amanda Smith, APRN-CNP

Amanda is a board-certified nurse practitioner with more than 10 years in healthcare. She earned both her Bachelor and Master of Science in Nursing from Chamberlain University.

Before aesthetics she worked as a registered nurse in neuro critical care and other ICU settings, including travel nursing in hospitals across Ohio, caring for patients with acute stroke and other high-acuity conditions. She was deeply passionate about that work and moved into aesthetic and wellness medicine for a more preventative, confidence-centered way of caring for people.

Her focus is natural-looking results, and a room where every patient feels comfortable, informed and confident in their own skin. She keeps training constantly, because safe, effective treatment depends on it.

@injectoramanda.np

Co-owner + founder

Joe Smith, MBA

Joe proudly serves as Elysium’s underpaid and overworked operations department: finances, negotiations, and fixing the many things Amanda somehow manages to break.

Outside Elysium, Joe is a Marine Corps veteran, a proud Ohio State alum with an MBA, and a Detective Sergeant working special investigations with a large Central Ohio law enforcement agency. He has coached football at every level and volunteers heavily for the mental health and personal transformation of veterans and first responders. He brings that same discipline and service to how Elysium runs.

The baby faces behind the brand

Since the second grade

Amanda and Joe grew up together, friends since about the second grade. At high school graduation he left for Marine Corps boot camp and told her he would come back to marry her. He did. They married a month after they both turned 19.

Today he runs the finances, keeps her on a budget and is her unpaid handyman, all while holding down his own demanding full-time job. Together they built a life, and a practice, they would choose in every lifetime.

Medical director

Dr. Phyllis Nsiah-Kumi, MD, MPH

Dr. Kumi earned her BS in Biology at Wheaton College, her MD at Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine, and a Master of Public Health at Northwestern University. She trained in Internal Medicine-Pediatrics and completed a fellowship in General Internal Medicine.

She has served as Chief Medical Officer for the U.S. Military Entrance Processing Station in Cleveland, Chief of Women’s Health at the VA Northeast Ohio Healthcare System, and Medical Director for Women Veterans Healthcare Services, and was named National Outstanding Civilian Employee of the Year by U.S. Military Entrance Processing Command. She now serves as Chief Women’s Health Officer at Bespoke Concierge MD.
