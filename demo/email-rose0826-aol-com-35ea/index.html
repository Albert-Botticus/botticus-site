Why parents choose a local sitter

Leaving your children with someone is a big decision. You should know who is coming, what they will do and how to reach them. That is why booking starts with a phone call.

Good babysitting is mostly simple things done reliably. Show up on time. Follow your routines for meals, bedtime and screens. Keep the kids safe. Tell you how the evening went.

What good babysitting looks like

Clear communication

You know the plan before you leave and can reach the sitter while you are out.

Your rules, your routine

Bedtimes, snacks, allergies and house rules are yours. A sitter should follow them.

Safety first

Emergency contacts, medical notes and a clear plan for the unexpected are set before you walk out the door.

A calm handoff

A short walkthrough at the start and a quick report at the end keep everyone on the same page.
