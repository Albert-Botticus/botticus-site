Booking guide

What to know before you book

A few minutes of preparation makes the whole evening easier.

Call 614-989-6251

Leave these for your sitter

Put them on the fridge or a note by the door.

  • Your phone numbers and a backup contact
  • Poison control: 1-
  • Allergies, medications and doctor's name
  • Bedtime, snack and screen rules
  • Home address for emergencies
  • Where the first aid kit and flashlight are

Questions worth asking any sitter

What experience do you have with my child's age?

Ages matter. A baby, a toddler and a school-age child need very different care.

How will you keep me updated?

Agree on texts, calls or a quick report at the end of the night.

What will you do in an emergency?

A good sitter knows to call 911 first, then you. Make sure your address is easy to find.
