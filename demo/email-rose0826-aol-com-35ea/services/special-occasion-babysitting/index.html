Plan ahead for big days

Weddings, holiday parties and long events change the usual routine. Children may be out late or in an unfamiliar place. A sitter helps them stay settled while you enjoy the day.

Popular dates fill quickly. Call as soon as you know your plans. Share the date, times, location and how many children need care.

If the sitter will be at your home, a hotel or a relative's house, tell us up front. We will go over the details with you.

Details to share when you call

  • Date, start time and expected end time
  • Where the children will be
  • Number of children and their ages
  • Any food, allergy or medical needs
  • How to reach you during the event
