Three levels of assurance

We provide compilation, review and audit services for nonprofit and for-profit companies. These are three different levels of service, and the right one depends on who will read the statements.

In general, a compilation presents your financial information in statement form without assurance. A review adds limited assurance. An audit gives the highest level. Lenders, grantors, boards and regulators often decide which level they require.

Several of our accountants have focused experience in nonprofit, church and governmental accounting, and in the audit of HUD for-profit and nonprofit organizations.

What to have ready

  • Prior-year financial statements, if any
  • Trial balance or accounting file for the period
  • Bank and loan statements
  • Any lender, grantor or board requirements for the statements
  • Names of the people who will read the statements

Questions about financial statements

How do I know whether I need a compilation, review or audit?

Ask your lender, grantor or board what they require. If you are unsure, contact us and we will help you work it out.

Do you serve nonprofits?

Yes. Our team has experience auditing nonprofit organizations, along with church and governmental accounting.
