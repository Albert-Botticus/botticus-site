Returns for individuals and businesses

We prepare tax returns for individuals, S corporations, C corporations, LLCs, partnerships, trusts, estates, company payroll returns and many state returns.

You will generally work with the same contact person for all your needs. That person learns your situation, so you do not start from scratch each year.

Tax work and accounting work go together. Because we also provide accounting and consulting, we can look at your return in the context of your whole financial picture.

What to bring

Exact needs vary. This list covers most situations.

  • Last year's tax return
  • Income documents such as W-2s, 1099s and K-1s
  • Business income and expense records or financial statements
  • Records of estimated tax payments made during the year
  • Mortgage interest, property tax and charitable giving records
  • Any notices received from the IRS or a state

Questions about tax returns

Do you prepare state returns?

Yes, we prepare many state returns. Contact us with your state and situation.

Can you prepare payroll returns?

Yes. We prepare company payroll returns.

Do you work with people outside Ohio?

Most of our clients are in Central Ohio, but we have clients throughout the country.
