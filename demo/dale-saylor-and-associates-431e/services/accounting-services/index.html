Accounting for many types and sizes of business

We provide a broad range of accounting services to many types and sizes of business. For some companies, we handle data entry and basic accounting at a lower overall cost than having employees do it.

If a key accounting employee leaves, we can bridge the period until you hire a replacement. For many companies, we fill the gap between the accounting staff and management. Some clients use us as their controller.

Because we also do tax work, we see the full picture. That background lets us finish the work in much less time than a single employee often could.

Ways clients use us

  • Data entry and basic accounting
  • Temporary cover when an accounting employee leaves
  • Cover while you refill a controller position
  • Ongoing controller services
  • Work in QuickBooks, Ajera, Pensoft Payroll or cloud-based platforms
