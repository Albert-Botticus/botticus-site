Why clients stay with us

Our clients tell us they value two things: a hands-on approach and a fast answer. When you contact us, a knowledgeable accountant responds to your question. You do not wait on a committee.

If you prefer to call, we answer the phone. You will generally work with the same contact person for all your needs. We pride ourselves on long-term relationships, and many of the services we offer exist because clients asked for them.

Help beyond the usual CPA work

Besides the traditional services of a CPA firm, we can fill accounting needs inside your business.

Cover a vacancy

If you lose a person from your accounting staff, including your controller, we can fill the gap while you hire a replacement.

Act as your controller

Some clients use us as their controller. Our broad accounting and tax background lets us do the work in much less time than an employee might.

Lower-cost data entry

For some companies, we handle data entry and basic accounting at a lower overall cost than doing it in house.

Common questions

Where are your clients located?

Most are in Central Ohio. We also have clients throughout the country.

Can you help if our bookkeeper just quit?

Yes. We offer services to bridge the period when a key accounting employee leaves, including a controller.

What kinds of tax returns do you prepare?

Individuals, S corporations, C corporations, LLCs, partnerships, trusts, estates, company payroll returns and many state returns.

Do you work with nonprofits?

Yes. We provide compilation, review and audit services for nonprofit and for-profit companies, and several of our accountants specialize in nonprofit work.
